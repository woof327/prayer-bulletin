# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, single page, no build step (user asked for HTML). Hosted from a git repository; remote URL to be supplied by the user.

## Users

새가족부 members (new church members and the team serving them) attending a 새가족부 기도회. They read along during the meeting, mostly on their own phones; some on laptops or tablets.

## Product Purpose

A digital 기도회 순서지 (order of service). It walks the room through: 찬양 1 → 섹션 1 → 찬양 2 → 섹션 2 → 찬양 3 → 섹션 3 → 축복찬양. Success: everyone finds the current step instantly and reads scripture and sheet music comfortably on a phone.

## Operating Context

Used live during the prayer meeting, often in a dimmed room during praise. People scroll in sequence and jump between steps. Sheet music must open full-screen and zoom on touch.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three prayer sections, each with 본문 (Nehemiah) and 연결본문 (linked passages), verbatim from the user (개역개정).
- Praise songs: 위러브 – 합심 / 위클레시아 – 함께 쌓아가는 / 영광에서 영광으로 (송재홍) / 감사해요 깨닫지 못했었는데 (축복찬양).
- Sheet music images not yet supplied: slots are placeholders until the user provides them.
- Content is scripture only ("본문만"): no added commentary, prayer topics, church name, date, or venue.

## Brand Commitments

User supplied a visual reference for the title treatment (heavy soft-serif display, deep maroon ink on off-white grain paper, engraved line illustration, small citation caption). Binding for the title design.

## Evidence on Hand

Scripture text and song list from the user. No sheet music yet. No church name, date, or logo; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The current step is always findable in one glance.
2. Scripture is set to be read slowly, not skimmed.
3. Add nothing the user did not give; the text speaks.
4. Works one-handed on a phone in a dim room.
